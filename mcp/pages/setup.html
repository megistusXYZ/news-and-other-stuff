Connect your Claude

  1. Press Add to Claude. Claude opens with the connector filled in; press Add.
  2. Claude asks you to Sign in with Nostr: approve once in your extension. It never signs or publishes as you.
  3. Come back here: the line below turns green.

What your Claude does each morning

  1. Reads what the people you trust posted in the last 24 hours, with your weather, teams and topics.
  2. Writes the front page.
  3. Hands it back to Brainstorm to check and keep.

Good to know

  • It runs on your Claude plan, about one conversation a day.
  • Brainstorm never sees your Claude login.
  • Every quote and link is checked against the posts before the paper is kept.
  • Disconnect any time, in Claude or in your settings here.

Editor's note: this is a local demonstration. On Brainstorm the page sits behind your sign-in and the connector lives at brainstormserver.nosfabrica.com/mcp; here a local token stands in for the Nostr signature.
