How it works

What your Claude does each morning

  1. Reads what the people you trust posted in the last 24 hours, with your weather, teams and topics.
  2. Writes the front page.
  3. Hands it back to Brainstorm to check and keep.

Good to know

  • It runs on your Claude plan, about one conversation a day.
  • Brainstorm never sees your Claude login.
  • Every quote and link is checked against the posts before the paper is kept.
  • Disconnect any time, in Claude or in your settings here.

A local demonstration: on Brainstorm this page sits behind your sign-in.
