The Observer · Getting started

Set up your daily paper

Three steps, once. After that your own Claude prints this paper every morning, from what the people you trust said, and hands it back to be checked and kept.

Checking your lens…

1

Connect your Claude

In Claude, open Settings, then Connectors, and add a custom connector with this address. Claude opens Brainstorm so you can sign in with Nostr.

Open Claude ↗

Waiting for Claude to connect…

2

Sign in with Nostr

It happens inside step one: a single signature with your extension lets your Claude read your digest and hand in pages.

Nothing else. It never signs or publishes as you, and never touches your follows, your trust settings or your scores.

3

Schedule the daily print

Print today's Nostr Observer with the Brainstorm connector. If my lens isn't ready, stop and tell me why.

Save them as a daily task at seven; your digest is ready from half past six.
  • Claude Code, the desktop app: a scheduled task, every day.
  • Claude Code on the web: a daily routine.
  • Or paste them into any Claude chat when you want a paper.

What your Claude does each morning

  1. Reads your digest: the last 24 hours, ranked by your web of trust, and your topics.
  2. Writes the front page.
  3. Hands it back to Brainstorm to check and keep.

Good to know

  • It runs on your Claude plan, about one conversation a day.
  • Brainstorm never sees your Claude login.
  • Every quote and link is checked against the posts before the paper is kept.
  • Disconnect any time, in Claude or in your settings here.

Editor's note: this is a local demonstration. On Brainstorm the page sits behind your sign-in and the connector lives at brainstormserver.nosfabrica.com/mcp; here a local token stands in for the Nostr signature.
