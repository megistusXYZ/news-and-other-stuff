## Found and fixed

1. **Sudoku could not be played from the keyboard.** Tabbing to a cell did
   not choose it, so digits and arrows did nothing; cells were announced only
   as "gridcell". Now a focused cell is chosen, each cell says "Row 1,
   column 4: empty" (or its digit, "given", or its notes), and the result
   line is announced.
2. **Five said nothing to a screen reader.** Now it is a named group ("Five,
   the daily word: type a five-letter word and press Enter") and each guess
   is read out ("CRANE: C not in it, R in the word, …").
3. **Captions and stories added details the posts do not say.** "From
   V3spering's garden in Montreal" (Montreal is never mentioned), "filed at
   breakfast … by mid-morning" (the €11 post was 15:38 UTC), "before
   breakfast in Europe" (10:06 UTC). Corrected in today's edition; the brief
   now forbids an inferred place, time of day or circumstance, and the daily
   task re-reads captions against their posts before resolving. Nothing
   automated checks this yet.
4. **Stop Press showed raw markdown** ("**BREAKING**"). `pressText` now reads
   bold, headings and links as their words (test added; living.js mirror
   byte for byte).
5. **Focus was lost after the skip link.** Closing a story after using the
   skip link left focus on the "Front page" heading; it now returns to the
   link that opened the story.
6. **Leftovers from the issue number.** The sudoku note said "No. 8FC05A"
   (now "edition 8FC05A"; the brief says so); the scheduled print's
   instructions did not mention the issue line (updated).

7. **The ‹ arrow had a hairline under it in Safari** (the house link rule's
   bottom border). The arrows now have none. Friday's copy (No. 1) was
   dressed before the arrows had styles, so its › is small and plain; only
   issues from No. 3 on carry the full folio.
